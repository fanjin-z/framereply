# FrameReply Roadmap

_Last reviewed: July 2026_

Priorities may change as the product and community develop. Planned work is not a
commitment to a particular feature, release, or delivery date.

## Delivered foundation

- Import screenshots or copied text through the app or Apple Shortcuts and
  preserve conversation history.
- Generate two user-reviewed reply ideas shaped by goals, personas, and chat
  context.
- Store data locally and connect directly to OpenAI or Z.ai with user-owned
  credentials and explicit consent.

## Now — App Store review

FrameReply has been submitted to the App Store and is pending review.

- Respond to App Review feedback and resolve release blockers.
- Ship and support the first public version.

## Next — Early post-launch improvements

- Turn early feedback into prioritized bug fixes and reliability improvements.
- Simplify onboarding and Shortcuts, and refine imports, conversation matching,
  and personalization controls.
- Add AI providers and models while improving compatibility, recovery, and cost
  clarity.
- Expand accessibility and localization as needs emerge.

## Vision — Longer-term direction

- Broaden communication workflows while keeping privacy boundaries clear and
  users in control of every reply.
