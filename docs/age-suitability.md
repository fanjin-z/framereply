# FrameReply Age Suitability

**App Store age rating: 13+.** Equivalent ratings may vary by country or region.

FrameReply is not designed specifically for children and is not listed in the App Store Kids category. It processes conversation text and screenshots selected by the user and generates suggested replies through FrameReply AI or a user-connected AI provider. Imported or generated text may infrequently contain profanity, references to alcohol, drugs, or medical issues, mature or suggestive themes, or non-explicit sexual content.

AI-generated content may be inaccurate, inappropriate, or unexpected. Review every suggestion before using or sending it.

Users must meet the applicable age and guardian-consent rules of Apple and the AI services they use. Users who connect their own provider must also meet that provider’s account and payment requirements. Some provider services may not be available to minors. FrameReply does not verify age or provide parental controls.

Only process content you are authorized to use. A minor's personal information must not be uploaded without all legally required authorization. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) and [Terms of Use](terms.md).
